    Sources and calculation notes

    Snapshot of the gaming.tools equipment database, skill builder, and specialization tracks, version 1.5.3.6, checked October 6, 2026. Funcom Chapter 3 notes describe augmentation and specialization.

    Coverage includes every personal garment, weapon, utility item and consumable in the source snapshot. Vehicles, building pieces, materials, ammunition and schematics are outside this character editor. Stillsuit bodies and radiation suits occupy chest and legs. Normal and unique tiers are distinct from community usefulness rankings.

    Ranks use cumulative source costs and connected skill-tree prerequisites. Skill points from Combat traits count only when purchased. Augmentation limits come from purchased Crafting traits. Only unique tier 6 garments and weapons can be augmented. Weapon-family augments are restricted to matching weapon tags. Roll position chooses a value within the source's min-to-max range; it is not a chance or a prediction.

    Armor is the sum of equipped body pieces. Each item's table shows base, grade, and augment contributions. Skills and progression show rank-specific modifiers with their sources. Combat activation, stacking between systems, final health and damage formulas need further verification and are not estimated. No live requests are needed to edit this snapshot.
