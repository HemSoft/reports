Source notes

S, A, B and C are Method's community usefulness ratings, with guide discussions dated 19 May 2026. They can vary by build and PvE/PvP use and may lag balance patches. Unrated schematics stay unranked; there are no D-rated entries in this pool. Augment grades depend on dungeon difficulty.

Snapshot checked 5 October 2026. Names, schematic status and station pools match game database 1.5.3.3, updated 24 September 2026. All 192 entries are schematics, including the tools and toxin-pill recipe.

Drop rates are database chest chances, not guaranteed drops or odds per full run. Shared schematics can have different rates at different stations.

JABAL Spitdart Focuser is listed as Ranger by Method. Matching effects and location support the alias; both names work in search. Disruptor M11 Heavy Kit uses the live loot table's B rating, despite A in Method's article text.
